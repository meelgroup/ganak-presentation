# Ganak: Engineering an Exact Model Counter

## Abstract

Model counting asks how many satisfying assignments a propositional formula has, and underpins probabilistic inference and quantitative verification. This talk presents Ganak, the exact model counter that won all but one award at the 2024–2026 Model Counting Competitions, and walks through all of its major parts: preprocessing, probabilistic component caching, the interplay of independent and optionally independent variables with a CDCL SAT solver, tree decomposition, and counting over arbitrary algebraic fields.

## Bio

Mate Soos works on SAT solving and model counting. He is the author of the SAT solver CryptoMiniSat, and co-develops the preprocessor Arjun, the approximate counter ApproxMC, and the exact counter Ganak with his long-time collaborator Kuldeep S. Meel. He previously worked in IT security, and currently works on the Solidity compiler for EVM-based blockchains such as Ethereum. He also maintains and develops hevm, a symbolic execution framework for EVM bytecode.
